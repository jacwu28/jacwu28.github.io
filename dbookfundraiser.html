Reasons why I chose this logo

Devin Booker often talks about how Kobe Bryant shaped the way he plays and thinks about the game. That’s why purple is everywhere in this design — it’s a clear tribute to Kobe and the mindset Booker brings to the court every night.

Even the little details tell a story. The “O” in Booker is turned into a book, a nod to his focus and attention to detail. His jersey number, #1, stands out proudly, a reminder that he’s the face of the Phoenix Suns.

To push the message further, a motivational quote from Booker is included — something to inspire players and fans to keep working hard and never give up.

If there’s one thing that could be better, it’s the color balance. Using so much purple makes the design feel a bit flat, and a splash of contrast could make the whole piece pop even more.

This fun image really humanizes Devin Booker, showing him with a big cartoon head, tiny body, and playful bunny ears. It’s a side of him fans rarely see—lighthearted, approachable, and full of personality—reminding us that even elite athletes can be playful and relatable.

Small touches, like his signature headband and the “Labubu” backdrop, keep it personal and recognizable. Together, they show that Booker isn’t just a star on the court—he’s also someone who can be charming, silly, and connect with fans in a way that feels genuine and fun.

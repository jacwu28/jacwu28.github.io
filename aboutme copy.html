Devin Armani Booker (English: Devin Armani Booker , October 30, 1996 - ), born in Grand Rapids, Michigan , USA , is an active American professional basketball player. On March 25, 2017, he scored 70 points against the Celtics, becoming the youngest 70-point man in history. He currently plays for the Phoenix Suns of the NBA and his main position on the court is shooting guard . Introduction Booker had an extraordinary basketball talent in his early years under the influence of his father , Melvin Booker , a professional basketball player . He entered the University of Kentucky in 2014 and was selected as the Southeastern Conference's Sixth Man of the Year in the 2014–15 season. He was also selected to the Southeastern Conference All-Rookie Second Team and the All-Rookie Team. In 2015, he was selected by the Phoenix Suns with the 13th pick in the first round of the draft . [ 1 ] In the 2016–17 season , Booker scored 70 points in a game between the Suns and the Boston Celtics , breaking his career single-game scoring record and the Suns' individual single-game scoring record. He also became the youngest player in league history to score 70 points. [ 2 ] Although Booker's athletic ability is average, his outside shooting ability is very accurate, and he is often regarded by the outside world as the future pillar of the Suns. Booker played in the NBA All-Star Game ( 2020 ) in place of the injured Damian Lillard . In the 2020-2021 season, Booker played well, averaging 24.7 points, 3.8 rebounds, and 4.3 assists per game. However, Booker was not selected as an All-Star reserve in the All-Star voting. However, after Anthony Davis was injured, he was selected as a substitute in the All-Star Game. Booker helped the Suns advance to the NBA Finals after 28 years [ 3 ] , but unfortunately they failed to win the championship. On July 7, 2022, Booker agreed to a four-year contract extension with the Suns with a maximum salary of $224 million; on the same day, he was announced as one of the cover stars of "NBA 2K23". On January 19, 2024, Booker scored 52 points in a 123–109 victory over the New Orleans Pelicans, 25 of which came in the first quarter. On January 24, in a 132–109 victory over the Dallas Mavericks, Booker shot 17-of-23 from the field, including 6-of-10 from three-point range, for 46 points. Two days later, against the Indiana Pacers, Booker scored 29 points in the first quarter, setting a Suns single-quarter scoring record. In a 133–131 loss, he shot 22-of-37 from the field and scored 62 points, becoming the ninth player in NBA history to score 60 or more points multiple times . [ 4 ] [ 5 ] National team career In June 2021, Booker was selected into the 12-man roster of the US men's basketball team for the Tokyo Olympics, and eventually won the gold medal . In the summer of 2024, Booker was selected to play for the US men's basketball team in the Paris Olympics, where he won the gold medal again . [ 6 ]
